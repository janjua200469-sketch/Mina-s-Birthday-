A LAST WORD FOR YOU

Today is your birthday, and somehow the world feels strangely beautiful to me because on this very day, years ago, it received the soul that would one day become my favorite part of existence. I look at you and wonder what kind of tenderness the universe must have gathered to create those doe-like eyes, that devastating smile, that laugh which makes something inside me bloom, those little giggles and blushes that undo me more completely than I could ever confess. But if I were given a choice between your beauty and the soul that lives beneath it, I would choose your soul a thousand times—your patience, your softness, your fire, your peculiar little ways, the warmth of your personality, every imperfectly perfect detail that makes you you. Your face captivates my eyes, your body captivates my senses, but your soul has taken possession of places within me that beauty could never reach. And tonight, while everyone celebrates the day you were born, I find myself quietly celebrating the far greater miracle of having lived in a world where I could find you at all. If I could place a gift at your feet, I would give you every star that ever kept me company while I missed you, every rose that ever reminded me of your tenderness, every word I have ever struggled to find for the magnitude of my love. Yet even all of that would feel miserably small. So I give you the only thing I truly possess—my devotion, my tenderness, my endlessly wandering heart—and I promise to keep discovering new reasons to love you long after this birthday has become another beautiful memory. Happy birthday, my love. Today the world celebrates your existence; I simply find myself grateful that your existence became part of mine.

— Safi ♡
